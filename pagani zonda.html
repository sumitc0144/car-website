Exterior package

The Pagani Huayra’s exterior package is a showcase of aerodynamic artistry and bespoke craftsmanship. It features full carbon fiber bodywork, often in exposed or tinted finishes, paired with aggressive elements like a front splitter, rear diffuser, and active aero flaps. Through Pagani’s Unico program, owners can personalize paint schemes, wheel designs, brake caliper colors, and even anodized trim details, turning each Huayra into a one-of-a-kind hypercar sculpture built for both speed and style.

Interior package

The Pagani Huayra’s interior package is a masterpiece of craftsmanship, blending futuristic design with classic luxury. Every detail is customizable through Pagani’s Unico program, allowing owners to select from exotic materials like crimson red leather, exposed titanium, carbon fiber, and polished aluminum. The cabin features analog dials, a gated manual shifter, and a layout that feels more like a jet cockpit than a car interior. From the seats to the dashboard, each element is sculpted with artistic precision, making the Huayra’s interior not just functional—but a work of automotive art.

Engine

The Pagani Huayra is powered by a 6.0L twin-turbo V12 engine from Mercedes-AMG, producing up to 850 hp and 1,000 Nm of torque. It’s rear-wheel drive, paired with a 7-speed automated manual transmission, and accelerates from 0–100 km/h in about 2.8 seconds with a top speed of around 385 km/h. Pure combustion, no hybrid tech—just raw, handcrafted performance.
